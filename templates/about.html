Processing Pipeline

4 sequential stages
Stage 1

Text Preprocessing & Normalization

Input text is converted to lowercase. Regular expressions strip external URLs (http\S+|www\.\S+), social user mentions (@\w+), and numerical digits. ASCII punctuation tables remove symbols, and standard English stopwords are filtered out to isolate informative lexical tokens.

Stage 2

Feature Extraction (TF-IDF)

The preprocessed tokens are mapped into a high-dimensional sparse matrix using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) over a fixed vocabulary of {{ '{:,}'.format(info.features) }} features. Both single words (unigrams) and adjacent word pairs (bigrams) are modeled with sublinear term-frequency scaling.

Stage 3

Linear SVM Classification

A multi-class Support Vector Classifier (LinearSVC) computes signed geometric distances to class-separating hyperplanes: \(s_k = \mathbf{w}_k^T \mathbf{x} + b_k\). The maximum margin determines the predicted category, optimized for high precision on sparse text data.

Stage 4

Word Influence & Explainability

Attribution weights are derived from the element-wise product of active feature weights and word frequencies for the winning class. Tokens producing the highest positive contribution are highlighted to provide immediate interpretability for human moderators.

Confidence Scores

Support Vector Machines optimize geometric decision margins rather than probabilities. The confidence percentages displayed across this interface are produced by applying a normalized softmax transformation to the raw margin scores, serving as relative proximity metrics rather than absolute Bayesian probabilities.

Limitations & Best Practices

  • Context & Sarcasm: Bag-of-words models analyze token co-occurrence without deep syntactic parse trees. Sarcasm, rhetorical negation, or reclaimed vernacular may occasionally trigger false positives.
  • Vernacular Drift: Online harassment slang evolves continuously. Periodic retraining on updated benchmarks is recommended.
  • Human Review: This tool is designed to assist human moderators and triage high-volume feeds, not to replace human discretion on edge cases.
